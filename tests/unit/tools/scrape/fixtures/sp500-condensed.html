List of companies in the S&P 500

For a list of previous components of the index, see Historical components of the S&P 500.

The S&P 500 is a stock market index maintained by S&P Dow Jones Indices. It comprises 503 common stocks which are issued by 500 companies with large market capitalizations, including two share classes of stock from three of its component companies: Alphabet, Fox Corporation and News Corp. The index includes about 80% of the American stock market by capitalization. It is weighted by free-float market capitalization.[1] An updated weighting of each component is available online.
